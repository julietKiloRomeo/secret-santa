# Reference: achievement texts (Dungeon Crawler Carl)

Saved for tone. Not project copy. Source: a fan post on the series' achievement texts.

Notable style points:
- A short in-world title, then a dry System AI line, then a REWARD line.
- The AI mocks the player, but the reward is often genuinely useful.
- Jokes land on a single specific action (the sign, the arrows, the shark) rather than a generic
  "good job".

Examples quoted in the post:

- "You've Discovered And Read An Official Dungeon Sign": "Wow. You can read. Whoopie."
- "You Monster!": "You have killed an infant! An infant!"
- "Total, Utter Failure.": "You failed a quest less than five minutes after you received it. Now that's talent."
- "Sushi Smoothie": "...Next time, use your foot."
- "Bisected": "Teleport mishaps are funny as shit."
- "That's Not How You Use Arrows, Dumbass.": "You took an arrow, and you physically stabbed someone with it."
- "Mentally Unstable Clothing Hoarder!": "You have over 500 of the exact same, stackable clothing item in your inventory."
- "PETA Enthusiast!": "The ghost of Steve Irwin smiles down upon you."
